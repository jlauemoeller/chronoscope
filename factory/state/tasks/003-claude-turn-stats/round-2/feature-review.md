## Summary
The Claude footer now counts the turn's distinct non-`<synthetic>` messages and measures wall-clock time from the turn's start to its latest `turn_duration` entry, with the fallbacks the spec and planner notes require. Both round-1 must-fixes are closed: mutation runs on a scratch copy show that the new tests fail when the count's dirty marking or the `prompt_ts` reset at a compaction without a boundary is removed.
